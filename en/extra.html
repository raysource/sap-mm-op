Once the main flow works, the cases you meet every day are these: goods that arrive with no purchase order, incoming material that fails inspection and goes back, stock moved between warehouses, invoices whose amount does not match the receipt, and a pile of GR/IR balances at month-end. This chapter takes each one apart and runs it.

1. Other goods receipt 501: how it differs from the 101 automatic PO

Spare parts, samples and urgent deliveries keep turning up without an order. Without an order you cannot post a 101, so you use movement type 501 (goods receipt without purchase order): stock increases, the value comes from the material's standard price, the offset goes straight to a consumption (expense) account, no GR/IR is created, and nothing appears in the three-way match at invoice verification.

Movement typeWhen to use itWhere the value comes fromOffsetting accountGR/IR
501Small deliveries with no order, spare partsMaterial standard price (S) or moving average (V)Consumption / expense account (GBB-VBR etc.)Not involved
561Initial stock load at go-liveMaterial standard priceOpening stock adjustment accountNot involved
101Normal goods receipt against an orderOrder net priceGR/IR clearingCreates a liability
There is another route: let the system create the orderSAP can generate a purchase order automatically on goods receipt without a PO, but 7 conditions must all hold: ① movement type 101 has "automatic PO" ticked (SPRO → Materials Management → Inventory Management → Goods Receipt → Create Purchase Order Automatically); ② the plant has a standard purchasing organization; ③ the vendor master has "automatic PO" ticked; ④ the source list is active at plant level; ⑤ the material has a source list record; ⑥ the material's purchasing view has "automatic PO" ticked; ⑦ the info record price is correct. Miss one and you get "No valid source found". In the exercises we use 501, which is faster than configuring those 7.

2. Returns: do not mix up 122 and 102

ScenarioTransaction / movement typeWhat it doesAccounting impact
Return a whole batch to the vendor (received, not yet invoiced)MIGO, movement type 122, with reference to the POStock decreases, GR/IR reversedGR/IR Dr / stock Cr
Wrong receipt, just reverse itMIGO, movement type 102, with reference to the material documentReverses the 101 in fullStock Cr / GR/IR Dr
Return after invoicingMR8M to reverse the invoice → then 122 → then ME22N to adjust the orderThe order mattersInvoice, stock and GR/IR roll back in sequence
The wrong order gets you stuckPosting a 122 against an invoiced order gives "Invoice already exists, return not possible". The correct sequence is MR8M to reverse the invoice → 122 to return the goods → ME22N to adjust the order quantity if needed. In the guided lab, always reverse before correcting, so the stock never disagrees with the books.

The old MB1A (goods issue), MB1B (transfer) and MB1C (other goods receipt) still work in S/4HANA, but SAP recommends MIGO for everything; batch input (BDC/CATT) is the exception, where the old transaction codes are often easier because MIGO's screen elements are more complex.

4. Invoice variance and blocking: tolerance quick reference (see the GR and IR chapter)

When the invoice amount differs from the receipt amount, SAP runs a tolerance check first. Tolerances are configured per company code in OMR6, and four keys cover most cases:

Tolerance keyWhat it checksSetting used hereWhat happens beyond it
BDSmall difference (absolute difference between invoice total and debit/credit)Upper limit 50 USDWithin the limit it posts to a small-difference account and the invoice still posts
DQQuantity deviation (invoice quantity vs received / ordered)Upper limit 1 PC or 0.5%Beyond it, a quantity variance is reported and the invoice is parked
PPPrice change (invoice unit price vs order net price)Upper limit 1%Beyond it, M8083 is raised and the invoice is stopped outright
VPPrice variance for moving-average materialsUpper limit 1%Beyond it the invoice is parked and the variance account needs manual handling
  1. MIR4 with the invoice number: check net amount, tax, gross amount, block status and both debit/credit lines.MIR4
  2. MRBR: lists every blocked invoice; release them one by one, or in bulk per vendor.
  3. F-53: payment only works after release; otherwise "Blocked invoices exist, release them in MRBR first".
  4. MR8M: for reversing a wrong invoice; only after reversal can you re-invoice or return the goods.

Invoice before receipt also worksBy default GR-based invoice verification requires the goods receipt before the invoice. To remove that restriction, change the info record in ME12 and untick GR-based IV, so the material's POs no longer carry the indicator; MIRO can then invoice first (GR/IR becomes a debit balance) and the receipt clears it. Useful for prepayments and for vendors who invoice before shipping.

6. MRP: how the net requirement is calculated

MD04 puts demand, stock and receipts on one timeline. For R-1001 in this course: demand 400 (production order 1000234, 2026-11-18) + safety stock 20 − available 300 = net requirement 120 PC. Available stock is summed at plant level (0001 + 0002), so a 311 does not change it; but if you had issued 20 PC with 261 first, available would be 280 and the net requirement 140 — MRP always works from the stock at the time it runs.
