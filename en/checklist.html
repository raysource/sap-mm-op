1. SAP GUI 8.10 basic operation

  • You can select a connection in SAP Logon and log on: client 110, user MMUSER01, language ZH
  • You can point to the five areas of the main screen: title bar / menu bar (system info at the right end) / toolbar (command field) / work area / status bar
  • You can distinguish the five command field variants MM03, /nMM03, /n, /oMM03 and /nex and say what each one does
  • You know the difference between Enter (check) and Ctrl+S / ✓ (save), and can say what happens to unsaved input after Enter
  • You use Tab to move between fields, F4 for the value list and F1 for a field's technical information (table name / parameter ID)
  • You can read the three status bar message types — green ✓ saved / yellow ! warning / red ✕ error — and say what to do next in each case
  • You have set LANG / WRK / BUKRS in SU3 and know it only changes your own defaults

4. Goods receipt without PO and GR/IR

  • MIGO 501 received 30 PC of R-1002: stock 20 → 50, amount 5,400.00, GR/IR unchanged
  • MB1B 311 moved 50 PC of R-1001 from 0001 to 0002: total still 300, no accounting document
  • MB1B 261 issued 20 PC to production order 1000234: stock down, consumption account up
  • MIRO deliberately invoiced 500.00 too much (0.23%): posted, but the invoice is blocked
  • Only after MRBR release can F-53 pay (payment before release is stopped)
  • MB5S totals 0.00 USD, and you can point at a line and say whether it is "received not invoiced" or "invoiced not received"
  • With all four switches on, MB01 with 101 and no PO receives 50 PC: the status bar shows both the material document and the automatically created PO
  • ME23N opens the generated PO with a net value of 9,000.00 USD (50 × 180.00) and the received quantity posted back
  • Switch off Automatic purchase order on the material and receive again on purpose: the system reports "Please enter purchase order" instead of quietly creating one
  • MIRO invoice 217,200.00 (+0.56 %) inside the PP tolerance: it posts, with 1,200.00 on the price variance account
  • MIRO invoice 226,800.00 (+5 %) beyond tolerance: M8083 is reported, the invoice is saved as blocked and reaches payables after MRBR release
  • After removing GR-based IV in ME12 a new PO can be invoiced in MIRO without a receipt; a later MIGO receipt brings MB5S back to zero
  • You can explain why goods dated this month cannot be received after MMPV has rolled over, and why both the M and S rows must be open in OB52
  • MD04 shows a net requirement of 120 PC, and running MRP creates a requisition with source MRP
  • You can explain the accounting difference between the three receipts 501 / 101 / 122
