The normal sequence is order first, receipt second. In reality someone always sends goods ahead: a verbal order, an urgent top-up, a vendor shipping on their own initiative. If the warehouse can only say "no order, no receipt", the pallet sits at the gate. SAP's answer is to let movement type 101 create the purchase order itself — provided four switches are on; miss one and you are back to keying an order number by hand.

1. How it works: the receipt looks up a source and creates the PO

In MB01 (goods movement) you choose movement type 101 and enter material / plant / quantity / vendor, leaving the purchase order field empty. The system then looks for a source in this order: the purchasing view of the material master → the source list of the plant → the price in the info record. If all three exist and all four "allow automatic creation" switches are on, the system creates a purchase order and posts the receipt against it, and the status bar shows both the material document number and the automatically created purchase order number.

One more implicit prerequisiteThe info record (ME11) must exist with the right price, otherwise the automatically created PO has no net price, the receipt is valued at 0 and the accounting document carries nothing. The source document puts it plainly: with the settings above in place, when you post a 101 receipt in MB01 without entering a purchase order, the system creates one for you — four switches + source list + price, not one of them optional.

Automatic PO creation only solves where the document comes from; the posting logic is still a standard receipt: stock up, GR/IR raised. When the invoice arrives in MIRO, GR/IR is cleared and turned into payables. If no invoice ever arrives for this PO, GR/IR stays open — which is exactly what the month-end GR/IR balance report (MB5S) has to explain.

5. Posting periods: why last month's goods cannot be received this month

A goods receipt writes to the material ledger and to accounting at the same time, so both periods must be open: the accounting period (OB52) and the material period (MMRV/MMPV). Forgetting to open the new period after closing the month is the wall beginners hit most often, and the error looks unrelated to the business.

The order of the two periodsOpen the material period first, then the accounting period (or both together). Open accounting only and the receipt reports "material period not open"; the other way round it reports "posting period not open". With both closed the accounting message comes first, so do not assume the material period is fine.

6. Pass criteria and common errors

  • MB01 with 101, no PO, R-1002 / 1710 / 0002 / 50 / 100124: after ✓ the status bar shows both the material document and the PO number.
  • ME23N opens the automatically created PO with the right quantity and net price (50 PC / 180.00 USD / 9,000.00 USD).
  • MMBE shows the stock of R-1002 in 0002 rising from 20 to 70 PC.
  • FB03 opens the accounting document: stock debit 9,000.00, GR/IR credit 9,000.00, balanced.
  • Switch off Automatic purchase order on the material and receive again on purpose: the system must report "Please enter purchase order" instead of quietly creating one.
  • Close MMPV into the next period and then post a receipt with this month's date: the system must report a material period error — the classic month-end symptom.

When not to use automatic PO creationAutomatic creation suits small, low-value, verbally ordered purchases. It bypasses purchasing approval and price comparison, so for large amounts, contracts or framework agreements insist on creating the PO before the goods arrive. Many companies simply switch automatic creation off for 101 and use 501 (goods receipt without purchase order) to expense such deliveries — the difference being that 101 with automatic creation still goes into stock and still creates GR/IR, while 501 goes straight to an expense and never touches stock.
