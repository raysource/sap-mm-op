SAP GUI 8.10 hands-on: learn the screen first, then run a purchase order on your own

The course has two halves. The first drills the SAP GUI 8.10 logon (Horizon theme), the five screen areas, the command field, shortcuts and the help system until they are muscle memory; the second moves into MM business and walks material master → purchase requisition → purchase order → goods receipt → invoice verification → payment, together with goods receipt without a PO and GR/IR reconciliation.

Every action can really be typed in the simulator on the page: enter a transaction code, press Enter, fill the fields, press ✓ to save — the system issues document numbers, raises errors and posts to the books. No SAP account and no virtual machine required.

The full chain behind one purchase order (with two branches)

MM01Master datamaterial / vendor / price→ME51NPurchase requisitioninternal demand→ME21NPurchase ordercommitment to the vendor→MIGOGoods receipt 101stock +, GR/IR −→MIROInvoice verificationGR/IR cleared, payables +→F-53Payment and clearingpayables −, bank −

Branch one (goods receipt without a PO, MB01): the goods arrive with no order, so the system uses the Automatic purchase order indicator on movement type 101, together with the matching switches in the material master, the vendor master and the source list, to create a PO and post the receipt against it. Branch two (IR before GR): switch off GR-based IV in the info record and you can invoice before the goods arrive. Both branches are shown screen by screen in the two Advanced chapters.

Five sentences to memorise before you start

  • Enter is not Save: Enter only runs checks and expands details; the step that writes to the database and issues a number is ✓ or Ctrl+S.
  • Incomplete master data means no documents: no purchasing view on the material → you cannot select it in a PO; no info record → the PO reports "No net price could be determined".
  • Goods receipt and invoice are two separate events: the receipt puts stock on the books and raises a GR/IR accrual liability; invoice verification converts that accrual into real payables.
  • Quantities and amounts must match: receive 300 and the invoice must also be 300; an amount difference beyond tolerance (1.000 % in this course) triggers M8083 or blocks the invoice.
  • Every stock change carries a document: either a material document only (311, value-neutral transfer) or a material document plus an accounting document (101 / 261 / 501).
