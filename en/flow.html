Step 2 — ME21N: requisition → purchase order

Saving the PO is not the end of itA saved order is still "not released", which does not block goods receipt in this course because no release strategy is configured. On real projects a purchase order often has to be released before goods may be received, either with ME29N or through the approval transactions, after which the status reads "released".

Step 4 — MIRO: three-way match, accrual becomes payables

What happens if the amount is more than 1% outEnter 219,000 (more than 1% above 216,000) → the status bar reports Price too high (exceeds tolerance limit 1.000 %) — message M8083 and the invoice will not post. Tolerances are configured per company code in OMR6 (BD small difference / DQ quantity deviation / PP price change / VP moving average variance). A small difference (≤ 1%) is allowed to post, goes to a small-difference account, and blocks the invoice, so payment requires MRBR to release it first.
